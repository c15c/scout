# What this needs to actually get used

## The wedge

Everyone already saves things they never do. Instagram saves, screenshots, group
chat links, open tabs. The wedge is not "another events directory" - it is
**"everything you saved, turned into a real date, place and price, ranked for
you."**

## Ten things that decide adoption

1. **Value before signup.** The feed renders on first load with a city default.
   Accounts are only required to save, connect a channel or get a nudge.
2. **Fifteen-second onboarding.** Three questions, skippable, shown once. Every
   later refinement happens through feedback on real cards, not a settings form.
3. **Paste-a-link as the hook.** One box that accepts an Instagram post, a TikTok
   video, a ticket page or a venue page and returns a dated, priced, mapped card.
   This is the shareable moment and the retention loop.
4. **Social-native ingestion.** Instagram and TikTok are where this content lives.
   Connect once, and saved posts become plans. See `SOCIAL-INGESTION.md`.
5. **Trust as a differentiator.** Every card shows the domain it came from, the
   date it was verified and a link to the exact page. Held-back items are visible
   with reasons. Competitors show stale, wrong, sponsored listings; being right is
   the moat.
6. **No dead ends.** Add to calendar, open in maps, share as a plan, invite a
   partner. A recommendation that cannot be acted on in one tap is wasted.
7. **Two-person mode.** Weekend decisions are rarely solo. A shared profile that
   merges two taste graphs is the strongest natural referral: inviting someone is
   the feature, not a growth hack.
8. **Notification discipline.** At most two a week: one "this looks like you" and
   one "the thing you saved is tomorrow". Quiet hours enforced. Nothing engineered
   for engagement.
9. **Local density over coverage.** One city where the coverage is genuinely
   complete beats twenty cities of scraps. Launch city by city, each seeded from
   council open data plus the twenty venues that matter.
10. **A free tier that is genuinely useful.** Free: feed, filters, save, calendar.
    Paid: connected social channels, partner profiles, unlimited pasted links,
    early notifications.

## What to measure

- Time from open to a saved item on day one.
- Share of listings with a verified deep link and a source image (target: 100%
  link, above 70% image).
- Suppression precision: how many held-back items were correctly held.
- Saves that turn into "I went".
- Pasted links per weekly active user - the clearest signal the wedge is working.
